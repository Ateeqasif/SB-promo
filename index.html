Designed Around Katy's Light

Katy and Cinco Ranch homes often feature generous windows and open floor plans. That brings in wonderful natural light, but it can also mean glare, heat and privacy become everyday considerations.

Solar and light-filtering shades can help manage strong afternoon sun while preserving your view. Blackout options can create darker bedrooms and more comfortable media spaces. Insulating shades can help moderate the temperature around your windows.

The right treatment depends on the window, the room and how you use it. That's where our in-home consultation makes a difference.
